United Nations Sustainable Development Goals Alignment

Empowering First-Year Engineering Students Against Cyber Exploitation

First-year B.Tech CSE students entering college are highly motivated to secure their first internship. Cyber syndicates capitalize on this optimism by sending deceptive emails demanding registration fees, training deposits, or personal identification. InternShield bridges this critical security gap.

⚖️ SDG 16: Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

Target 16.4 & 16.5: Combating organized cybercrime, illicit financial flows, and predatory student extortion.

InternShield strengthens student institutional resilience by demystifying deceptive email tactics and automating verification against university placement standards.

💼 SDG 8: Decent Work & Economic Growth

Target 8.6: Substantially reduce the proportion of youth not in employment, education, or training by protecting legitimate pathways.

Genuine internships provide fair compensation and professional mentorship. By filtering out fee-charging scams, students invest their time only in high-value, authentic technical opportunities.

🏛️ Indian Cyber Law & Statutory Provisions

Information Technology Act, 2000:

  • Section 66D: Punishment for cheating by personation using computer resources (imprisonment up to 3 years and fine).
  • Section 66C: Identity theft using stolen credentials, OTPs, or signatures.
  • IPC Section 420: Cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property.
